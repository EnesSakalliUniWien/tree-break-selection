## Corrections in priority order

1. **Describe the two edge/sibling spectral contexts, and separate MP from the actual retained rank.** In `manuscript/sections/method/edge_test.tex:93–118,146–197`, the all-leaf parent PCA, column-standardized Pearson representation, and filled-out orthonormal complement describe a different procedure. `child_parent_divergence_annotation.py:84–119` supplies separate basis and test rows for the edge and returns the full descendant context for siblings. `projection/spectral/tree_estimator.py:360–387` fits edge-basis null means and continuous whitening from the basis rows, with the continuous within-child calculation subject to its fallback. `projection/spectral/marchenko_pastur.py:98–159` uses model-null-whitened tangent rows and the leaf-row count in the MP aspect ratio. The spectral floor is applied to the raw spike count and bounded by available positive directions, with numerical eigenvalue-group extension (`projection_dimension_estimators.py:146–168`). The basis helper slices available PCA rows and rejects insufficient input (`projected_wald_projection_basis.py:10–52`). The optional contrast-energy path can replace a positive nominal rank when enabled (`projected_wald_kernel.py:73–85,103–139`), so report the configured mode and distinguish nominal rank, actual retained rows, and reported reference df. Also revise `manuscript/sections/implementation/section.tex:46–49`, `assumptions_validation.tex:29–39,72–75`, and `wiki/analyses/local-marchenko-pastur-rule.md:38–59,118–148`. The MP denominator can differ from the covariance divisor if internal rows are included. The field named `effective_independent_rows` is a leaf-row count, not a dependence estimate.

2. **Keep the fixed-membership leaf-only PCA result empirical.** Replace the claimed independence explanation in `wiki/analyses/selected-pca-projected-wald-validation.md:80–87`. For fixed groups with means separated by `Δ`, the total centered scatter is the pooled within-group scatter plus `n_L n_R/(n_L+n_R) Δ Δᵀ`. Even leaf-only PCA can therefore use the tested contrast. The locked June runs (`:24–69`, JSON and CSV cited at `:91–99`) show approximate null rejection at 0.05 only for the three tested fixed-membership Gaussian settings. Their sizes and covariance conditions exclude the two-leaf mechanism exposed by the cancer-PC diagnostic. Qualify the categorical wording about internal rows at `:102–105,116–117`, `wiki/analyses/local-marchenko-pastur-rule.md:52–56`, and `wiki/analyses/method-proof-web.md:203–207`. The edge-basis context excludes internal rows. The full-leaf sibling context excludes them by default and supports an opt-in append path (`orchestrator.py:316,466`, `child_parent_divergence_annotation.py:84–101`, `tree_estimator.py:363–368,416–425`). Treat the June 16 internal-row MP threshold behavior at `local-marchenko-pastur-rule.md:229–236` as historical.

3. **Label the active reference correctly.** In `manuscript/sections/method/edge_test.tex:194–197,307–317` and `assumptions_validation.tex:19–27`, state the isotropic Gaussian and fixed/conditionally independent projection requirements alongside the nominal `χ²_k` reference. Orthonormality alone supplies no null law. Edge basis rows and test rows are partitioned deterministically within fitted children. Fixed membership and independent input rows give one separation argument, but a tree selected from those same data conditions on both halves. Fitted test covariance and any contrast-energy rank selection still require their own laws. Contrast covariance geometry is model-based, with separate branch policy and continuous-covariance choices. Revise `edge_test.tex:48–62` to distinguish default sampling-only variance, opt-in normalized branch-time variance, and the fitted continuous covariance policy. The toy example's number is an illustration for one retained direction.

4. **Treat sibling calibration as a weighted working model.** In `manuscript/sections/method/sibling_test.tex:230–236,287–315`, replace claims of accounting for optimism or recovering an internal null with the actual estimator and its limitations. Positive weights use observed edge decisions, and both untested/blocked child edges can supply weight one. The estimator's weighted chi-square likelihood is a working objective for dependent, potentially non-null records. The active estimator can use a family baseline and cap specific large-parent extrapolations (`empirical_null_inflation_estimation.py:530–581,629–647`). Group-level concentration and leave-one-group checks measure support. They establish neither independence nor null status. The current cancer-PC diagnosis identifies 11 mean-different records among 494 supported records in the selected 16/480 root, contributing 0.708302 of the weighted numerator, with only two stopping-event groups. Removing those records is a generator-oracle intervention. The retained records from these selected signal datasets do not estimate population Type I error.

5. **Mark independent exact F as a mathematical reference and retired mode.** `manuscript/sections/method/sibling_test.tex:398–420` says that an exact mode is available. It was removed on 2026-10-16 (commit `7cc4e1c0`). `assumptions_validation.tex:50–53` and `wiki/analyses/empirical-null-calibration-reference-law-contract.md:42–49` already record its absence. The independent common-scale ratio has the stated F law under fixed unit weights. Taking the maximum with the raw chi-square tail can only make the resulting p-value more conservative. Support-group counts, Kish ESS, and overlapping empirical weights cannot substitute for the independent chi-square denominator.

6. **Identify multiplicity algorithms and selected error measures separately.** Change `README.md:103–104` from 'multiplicity control' to the implemented Tree-BH adjustment. `manuscript/sections/method/edge_multiplicity.tex:5–11` should distinguish its algorithmic recurrence from a theorem for fitted-tree p-values. `sibling_test.tex:422–426` needs the per-depth eligible edge-reachable frontier rather than a single family of all focal pairs. See `tree_bh/correction.py:41–86` and `inflated_projected_wald_annotation/fdr_annotation.py:82–182`. A separate argument is required for each inferential target, including edge FDR, sibling selected-family FDR, and false splits in final traversal.

7. **Retain the selective prototypes at their documented scope.** `wiki/sources/conditional-diffusion-monte-carlo-evidence.md:33–89` already distinguishes numerical event replay from exact-real region certification and records acceptance failures. The fixed-budget rank argument is conditional on exact event evaluation and exchangeability under the specified conditional model. The selected-root model and small-benchmark examples do not establish validity for estimated covariance, arbitrary diffusion eigenspaces, all descendant histories, or biological cancer data. This page's limitations should travel with downstream summaries.

8. **Keep the oracle claim at fixed K.** `wiki/analyses/method-proof-web.md:228–237` bounds outputs only within the oracle's cut family, including the exact number of clusters K. The interpretation at `:239–241` should retain that condition. A traversal output with a different number of clusters belongs to another comparison family.

## Evidence limits

- Active defaults and registered benchmark overrides must be reported separately. Enabling internal barycenters or contrast-energy rank changes the statistical object.
- A positive-dimensional test produced by the kernel supplies nominal reference metadata. Selection-aware validity depends on the null, covariance estimator, event, and requested error criterion.
- The focused historical test records establish only their stated implementation contracts. No literature result or archived experiment establishes end-to-end selected-tail calibration.
- The external source scope is recorded in `raw/inbox/tbs-knowledge-primary-sources-20261104.md`.
