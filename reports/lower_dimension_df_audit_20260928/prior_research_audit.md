# Prior research audit: projection dimension and degrees of freedom

## Main conclusion

The earlier work does not establish an integer df bookkeeping error; it does establish failures of the nominal reference law in adaptive settings. For a standard Gaussian whitened contrast `z ~ N(0,I)` and a fixed, row-orthonormal `k × p` projection P, `||Pz||² ~ chi_square(k)` exactly at any finite dimension. Thus eigenvalues do not weight this reference, and the Satterthwaite match has scale 1 and df k. The current kernel returns df equal to the projected vector length. The unresolved issue is whether the supplied projection and its rank are independent of the tested contrast after data-dependent selection. When rank is selected using contrast energy, or the same evidence selects the hierarchy and focal sibling, reporting df k does not by itself give a chi-square null law. See the [reference-law implementation](/Users/berksakalli/Projects/kl-te-cluster/tree_break_selection/hierarchy_analysis/statistics/projection/projected_wald/projected_wald_reference_distribution.py:10), [kernel contract](/Users/berksakalli/Projects/kl-te-cluster/tree_break_selection/hierarchy_analysis/statistics/projection/projected_wald/projected_wald_kernel.py:48), and the open-question record at [Q12–Q13](/Users/berksakalli/Projects/kl-te-cluster/wiki/questions/open-mathematical-questions.md:1217).

## Earlier simulation evidence

- The locked 2026-06-01 fixed-membership Gaussian study used 1,000 replicates per setting and selected both PCA rows and MP dimension from local null data. With descendant leaves only, nominal 0.05 rejection was 0.045–0.050 across 8-, 16-, and 32-dimensional identity/AR(1)/ill-conditioned settings. Appending deterministic child-mean rows raised it to 0.648, 0.988, and 1.000. Every setting had mean test dimension 2 and raw MP count 0, so the discrepancy was not a df change or false MP spike count. The 2026-06-05 rerun reproduced the leaf-only rates at 0.046–0.059 and the child-mean failures at 0.671–1.000. These are local fixed-membership results, not full-tree validation; tree construction, focal-node selection, sibling FDR, and traversal were excluded. - A distinct 2026-06-13 decomposition used a fixed topology and compared a projection learned from the tested sample with one learned from independent tree data and a random fixed orthonormal projection. The wiki records 20 replicates: same-sample adaptive tails were 0.916 for df 0–1 and 0.745 for df 1–2; the independent tree-sample controls were 0.053 and 0.076, and random fixed-basis controls were 0.063 and 0.036. It also records operator Satterthwaite scale 1 and df equal to projection dimension. This is consistent with selection dependence changing the law, not an algebraic df error. ## Previous isolated Wald-rank result

The 2026-09-28 isolated Wald-rank reassessment directly tested small models with independent standard Gaussian contrasts in dimensions 2 and 4. At adaptive energy fraction 0.90 and alpha 0.01, held-out rejection was 0.014464/0.014595 for distinct spectrum `(2,1)` and 0.015531/0.015342 for partially tied `(4,2,2,1)` across two one-million-contrast seeds. All 16 distinct/partially tied held-out adaptive cells were above target after Holm correction across 216 cells. Fully tied `(1,1)` was 0.009949/0.009993 after complete-eigenspace retention; that repair addressed rotation dependence there but did not fix rank selection across distinct groups. The supplied spectra label rank-selection groups; they are not covariance eigenvalues of the simulated null. Fixed-rank controls were not flagged, including fixed cutoffs and full-rank projections. This is direct evidence that contrast-selected rank can break the ordinary chi-square-k tail in tiny models; it does not establish a wrong fixed-rank df or explain the full-pipeline failures, since full-pipeline methods disable adaptive energy rank. See [experiment details](/Users/berksakalli/Projects/kl-te-cluster/reports/eigenspace_reassessment_20260928/wald_rank/README.md:1), [held-out results](/Users/berksakalli/Projects/kl-te-cluster/reports/eigenspace_reassessment_20260928/README.md:115), and [frozen design](/Users/berksakalli/Projects/kl-te-cluster/reports/eigenspace_reassessment_20260928/wald_rank/design.json:1). The June leaf-only PCA results do not conflict: those used fixed sibling membership, covariance estimation, leaf-only PCA/MP rank selection at its k_min=2 floor, and local contrast construction; this isolated September experiment directly selects rank from the tested contrast with prescribed spectra.

## Rank-selection history and status

The May controlled Marchenko–Pastur probe found 8–13% finite-sample top-spike exceedance rates in small Gaussian matrices; the MP edge was treated as an asymptotic rank heuristic, not an exact calibration rule. Q17’s June dimension grid showed the selected sibling dimension varied materially across candidate rules, and the broader 2026-06-05 sweeps found that rank changes alone did not repair selected-tree false splits without losing signal. These are dimension-policy diagnostics, not evidence that the fixed-projection df should use PCA eigenvalue weights. The distinction between raw MP count and floored test dimension is explicit in the [MP analysis](/Users/berksakalli/Projects/kl-te-cluster/wiki/analyses/local-marchenko-pastur-rule.md:119); Q17 remains open in the [dimension-rule record](/Users/berksakalli/Projects/kl-te-cluster/wiki/sources/mp-projection-dimension-behavior-sweeps-20260605.md:29).

The prior research therefore supports a small-model decomposition that holds the Gaussian null and orthonormality fixed while separately varying: (1) fixed `P, k`; (2) `P, k` learned on independent training data; (3) same-data PCA with fixed rank; and (4) same-data contrast-energy rank selection. Rebuild the tree only as a separate final arm. Record actual retained rank and verify `P P.T = I_k` in each arm. This isolates a df implementation error from selection-induced tail distortion; existing fixed-membership evidence does not close the full selected-tree question.
