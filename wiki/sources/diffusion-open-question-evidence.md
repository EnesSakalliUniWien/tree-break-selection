# Diffusion Open Question Evidence

## Summary

Three Sol agents answered six open questions using live source, primary
mathematical references and bounded numerical probes. They specified the
conditional radial law, demonstrated full-history re-entry in one dimension,
excluded direct PSD Toeplitz Vandermonde applicability, and separated root
validity from covariance, fitted-scale and traversal guarantees.

## Key Points

- The full-history event requires refitting the entire diffusion constructor.
  Outer median epsilon still contains internal NNKDE/BGH adaptation.
- A nine-row, one-feature Gaussian example preserves the observed sequence at
  radius ratios 1 and 1.2 but not 1.1. Its root partition remains unchanged.
- Four localized transitions and a finite initial panel are retained; neither
  establishes exhaustive boundary enumeration or exact endpoints.
- Newton is a local refinement tool. Certified global coverage needs branch,
  tangency, jump and tail treatment, plus conditional integration error bounds.
- The actual nonzero symmetric zero-diagonal diffusion operator cannot be PSD;
  the separately tested PSD coordinate Gram also fails Toeplitz structure.
- Fitted statistics can be calibrated by their fully replayed level sets under
  a justified conditional path law. Plug-in chi-square tails are different.

## Evidence

The three raw notes contain inspected source paths, primary-source links,
equations, probe code, captured results and explicit unresolved questions.
The parent independently reproduced re-entry through uninstrumented production
calls, verified unchanged root partitions at all three radii, and checked the
recorded diffusion and installed-kernel source hashes.
