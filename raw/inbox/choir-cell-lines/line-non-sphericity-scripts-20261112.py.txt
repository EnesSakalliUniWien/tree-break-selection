# ===== protocol.md =====
# # Protocol: what makes the cells of one cell line non-spherical (frozen 2026-11-12, before any run)
#
# ## Why
#
# On the CHOIR cells (48,879 cells, 30 PCs, 190 cell lines) the cells of a single cell line are far from
# spherical: within one line the largest eigenvalue is 7.0 to 94.1 times the median eigenvalue (175 lines with at
# least 100 cells), against a 99% point of 1.30 to 2.93 for one spherical Gaussian population. A count of
# eigenvalues above a spherical edge is therefore large inside one line (5 to 12 with `invariant_directions`, 6 to
# 27 with the prototype rule), and the prototype rule for k failed on these cells (wiki page `k-margin-prototype`).
#
# Question: where does the non-sphericity inside one cell line come from, and can it be removed without the
# cell-line labels?
#
# ## Data and definitions (frozen)
#
# - Cells: `results/analyses/choir_cell_lines_tbs_lbfgsb_20261106/pca.npy` (48,879 x 30; PCA of
#   log1p(CPM / 100) on 2,000 scaled highly variable genes, see `preparation.json`) and `cell_metadata.csv`
#   (`Cell_line`, `pool_id`, `source_library`).
# - Lines: the 175 cell lines with at least 100 cells. For a line with cells X (n x 30): S its covariance
#   (divisor n - 1); T1 = largest eigenvalue / median eigenvalue; k current = number of rows of
#   `invariant_directions(X)`; k prototype = number of rows of `directions_with_margin(X)` from
#   `results/analyses/k_margin_prototype_20261112/k_rule.py`; q = `null_quantile(n, 30)`.
# - Pooled within-line covariance W: sum over lines of (n_M - 1) S_M divided by the sum of (n_M - 1), over all
#   190 lines. W without line L leaves L out of both sums.
# - "Whitened by C": centred cells times the symmetric inverse square root of C.
# - Tree: `results/analyses/spr_alternatives_best_flat_cut_20261111/tree_tbs_family_diffusion_markov_ward.npz`
#   (`parent`, `leaf_row`; node index below 48,879 is a leaf; helper `index_tree` / `subtree` in that folder's
#   `spr_lib.py`). Called nodes and their recorded k: `results/analyses/spr_refit_20261111/k15_base_splits.json`
#   (current rule) and `results/analyses/k_margin_choir_20261112/kmargin_splits.json` (prototype rule).
#
# ## Part A: the lines, with labels (`a_lines.py`, outputs `a_lines.txt`, `a_lines.json`)
#
# - A0 self-checks (asserts): the k of the 175 lines reproduces `results/analyses/k_margin_choir_20261112/cause.txt`
#   (current rule {5: 1, 6: 1, 7: 18, 8: 48, 9: 46, 10: 45, 11: 13, 12: 3}; prototype rule as printed there);
#   2,000 draws of one Gaussian population with covariance W and 238 cells, whitened by W, give k prototype >= 1
#   in 8 to 36 draws.
# - A1 the profile in PC coordinates: per PC the median over lines of the within-line variance and of its share
#   in the total variance of that PC; per line the share of off-diagonal energy in S (1 minus the sum of squared
#   diagonal entries over the squared Frobenius norm).
# - A2 common covariance: every line whitened by W without that line: T1, k current, k prototype.
# - A3 sequencing depth: depth per cell recovered from the CPM matrix as in
#   `results/analyses/depth_confound_20261107/`; per line the share of the within-line variance (trace of S)
#   explained by a linear fit of every PC on log depth; the correlation of the first principal score of the line
#   with log depth; T1 and both k after removing that fit within the line; and the same after removing the fit
#   and then whitening by the pooled within-line covariance of the depth-removed cells without that line.
# - A4 pools: lines whose cells come from more than one `pool_id` or `source_library`; for those the share of
#   the within-line variance explained by the pool means.
# - A5 what remains after A2: number of lines with k prototype 0, 1, 2 and more; T1 against q.
#
# Predictions, stated before any run:
# - PA1 the common covariance carries most of it: after A2 the median T1 over lines is below 5 (25.6 before).
# - PA2 after A2 most lines still exceed the spherical 99% point: k prototype >= 1 in more than half of the lines.
# - PA3 depth alone carries little of it: after removing the depth fit the median T1 stays above 10.
#
# ## Part B: without labels, from the tree (`b_tree.py`, outputs `b_tree.txt`, `b_tree.json`)
#
# - B1 a label-free common covariance: for m in (30, 50, 100), the maximal subtrees of the Markov/Ward tree with
#   at most m cells and at least 5; each centred at its own mean; W_tree(m) = pooled covariance over those
#   subtrees. Reported: number of subtrees, cells covered, share of subtrees with at least 90% of their cells
#   from one line, and the eigenvalues of W^-1/2 W_tree(m) W^-1/2 (smallest, median, largest), W being the
#   label-based pooled within-line covariance.
# - B2 the 175 lines whitened by W_tree(m): T1, k current, k prototype, for each m.
# - B3 the 1,838 called nodes, whitened by W_tree(50): k prototype and k current of each node's cells, beside
#   the recorded k in PC coordinates, by size class (fewer than 15, 15 to 31, 32 to 99, 100 to 999, 1,000 and
#   more): k = 0, k above 15, median k. And for called nodes with at least 40 cells: nodes with at least 90% of
#   their cells from one line against all other nodes: share with k prototype >= 1 and median k, in PC
#   coordinates and whitened.
# - B0 self-checks (asserts): the cells under the root are all 48,879; the recorded k of 20 randomly chosen
#   called nodes is reproduced in PC coordinates under both rules; W recomputed equals Part A's definition (the
#   script computes it itself and prints its trace and largest eigenvalue for comparison).
#
# Predictions, stated before any run:
# - PB1 W_tree(50) agrees with W within a factor 2 in every direction (all eigenvalues of W^-1/2 W_tree W^-1/2
#   between 0.5 and 2).
# - PB2 whitened by W_tree(50), k prototype is at most 15 at all 103 called nodes with at least 1,000 cells.
# - PB3 whitened by W_tree(50), nodes inside one line have k prototype >= 1 less often than the other nodes.
#
# ## Rules for every part
#
# - Only files in `results/analyses/line_non_sphericity_20261112/` are written. No production file, test, wiki
#   page or raw capture is changed; nothing is committed, installed or downloaded.
# - Run pattern: `cd /Users/berksakalli/Projects/kl-te-cluster && OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 uv run
#   --no-sync --offline python <script>`; scripts pass `uv run --no-sync --offline ruff check`.
# - This protocol is frozen. Anything done differently goes into `deviations_a.md` or `deviations_b.md` with the
#   reason; a failed assert or prediction is reported, not removed.
# - Results are counts with totals, or medians with minimum and maximum. Each part ends with what it did not
#   check. No explanation is given without a measurement that supports it.


# ===== deviations_a.md =====
# # Deviations and open choices, Part A (`a_lines.py`)
#
# No assert failed; no assert or prediction was changed. PA1 was not held (median T1 after A2 13.774, not below 5).
#
# ## Choices the protocol left open
#
# 1. A0 null draws: seed `np.random.default_rng(20261112)`; cells drawn as N(0, W) through the Cholesky factor
#    of W, centred, then whitened by the symmetric inverse square root of the same W. Result: 33 of 2000 draws
#    with k prototype >= 1 (inside 8 to 36, near the top).
# 2. A0 also asserts the median T1 in PC coordinates (25.64, as in `k_margin_choir_20261112/cause.txt`).
# 3. W and every pooled covariance use all 190 lines (protocol); the 175 lines with at least 100 cells are the
#    lines reported.
# 4. A1 "total variance of that PC": variance (divisor n - 1) over all 48,879 cells.
# 5. A3 depth: no per-cell depth file was saved in `depth_confound_20261107/` (only `pcs.npy`, assignments and
#    summaries), so depth is recomputed exactly as in `depth_confound_20261107/real.py::recovered_counts`:
#    library = 1e6 / smallest non-zero CPM of the cell, counts = CPM x library / 1e6, integrality asserted
#    (< 1e-6), rounded; depth = row sum of the rounded counts. Cell order of the h5ad was asserted equal to
#    `cell_metadata.csv` (the canonical reordering in `real.py` is not applied, it is not needed here).
# 6. A3 depth fit: ordinary least squares of all 30 PCs on [1, log depth], fitted separately within each line
#    (all 190 lines, so the pooled depth-removed covariance covers the same lines as W). Share explained =
#    1 - trace(S of residuals) / trace(S).
# 7. A3 correlation: Pearson correlation of the score on the leading eigenvector of S with log depth; the
#    eigenvector sign is arbitrary, so the summary is over the absolute value (signed value in `a_lines.json`).
# 8. A4: every line comes from exactly one `pool_id` and from more than one `source_library` (all 190), so "the
#    pool means" are the `source_library` means within the line; share = between-library sum of squares over
#    the within-line sum of squares (traces). By `pool_id` the share is 0 for every line.
# 9. A5 "T1 against q": count of lines with T1 > q and the distribution of T1 / q, with q = `null_quantile(n, 30)`.

# ===== a_lines.py =====
"""Part A of protocol.md: where the non-sphericity inside one cell line comes from (with labels)."""
import json
import sys
from collections import Counter
from pathlib import Path
from time import perf_counter

import anndata as ad
import numpy as np
import pandas as pd
from scipy import sparse

HERE = Path(__file__).resolve().parent
ROOT = HERE.parents[2]
sys.path.insert(0, str(ROOT))
sys.path.insert(0, str(HERE.parent / "k_margin_prototype_20261112"))

from k_rule import directions_with_margin, null_quantile  # noqa: E402

from tree_break_selection.hierarchy_analysis.statistics.sibling_divergence.split_count_rule_out import (  # noqa: E402
    invariant_directions,
)

START = perf_counter()
SOURCE = HERE.parent / "choir_cell_lines_tbs_lbfgsb_20261106"
metadata = pd.read_csv(SOURCE / "cell_metadata.csv", index_col=0, dtype=str,
                       usecols=["cell_id", "Cell_line", "pool_id", "source_library"])
pcs = np.load(SOURCE / "pca.npy")
N, P = pcs.shape
groups = {line: index for line, index in metadata.groupby("Cell_line").indices.items()}
LINES = sorted(line for line, index in groups.items() if len(index) >= 100)
out_lines = {line: {"n": int(len(groups[line]))} for line in LINES}
report = []


def say(text=""):
    print(text)
    report.append(text)


def spread(values):
    values = np.asarray(values, dtype=float)
    return f"median {np.median(values):.3f} (min {values.min():.3f}, max {values.max():.3f})"


def k_table(values):
    return dict(sorted(Counter(int(v) for v in values).items()))


def scatter(x):
    centred = x - x.mean(axis=0)
    return centred.T @ centred


def inverse_root(c):
    values, vectors = np.linalg.eigh(c)
    return vectors @ np.diag(values ** -0.5) @ vectors.T


def describe(x):
    """T1, k current, k prototype of cells x."""
    values = np.linalg.eigvalsh(np.cov(x.T))[::-1]
    return float(values[0] / np.median(values)), len(invariant_directions(x)), len(directions_with_margin(x))


def run_step(name, cells_of):
    """Apply describe to every line (cells_of(line) gives its cells), store per line, print summary."""
    rows = {line: describe(cells_of(line)) for line in LINES}
    for line, (t1, current, prototype) in rows.items():
        out_lines[line][name] = {"T1": t1, "k_current": current, "k_prototype": prototype}
    t1, current, prototype = (np.array(column) for column in zip(*rows.values()))
    say(f"{name}: T1 {spread(t1)}")
    say(f"{name}: k current {k_table(current)}")
    say(f"{name}: k prototype {k_table(prototype)}")
    return t1, current, prototype


def pooled(points, all_groups):
    """Sum of within-group scatter and sum of (n - 1) over every group (all 190 lines)."""
    parts = {line: scatter(points[index]) for line, index in all_groups.items()}
    return parts, sum(parts.values()), sum(len(index) - 1 for index in all_groups.values())


def without(parts, total, dof, line):
    return (total - parts[line]) / (dof - (len(groups[line]) - 1))


# A0 self-checks
say("== A0 self-checks ==")
t1_before, k_current_before, k_prototype_before = run_step("A0 PC coordinates", lambda line: pcs[groups[line]])
assert len(LINES) == 175
assert k_table(k_current_before) == {5: 1, 6: 1, 7: 18, 8: 48, 9: 46, 10: 45, 11: 13, 12: 3}
assert k_table(k_prototype_before) == {6: 1, 8: 2, 9: 3, 10: 3, 11: 11, 12: 15, 13: 18, 14: 26, 15: 25, 16: 21, 17: 18,
                                       18: 12, 19: 6, 20: 6, 21: 4, 22: 1, 23: 1, 25: 1, 27: 1}
assert round(float(np.median(t1_before)), 2) == 25.64
say("assert passed: k current and k prototype of the 175 lines reproduce cause.txt; median T1 25.64")
parts, total, dof = pooled(pcs, groups)
W = total / dof
rng = np.random.default_rng(20261112)
root_w = np.linalg.cholesky(W)
w_inverse_root = inverse_root(W)
null_hits = 0
for _ in range(2000):
    draw = rng.normal(size=(238, P)) @ root_w.T
    null_hits += len(directions_with_margin((draw - draw.mean(axis=0)) @ w_inverse_root)) >= 1
say(f"Gaussian population with covariance W, 238 cells, whitened by W: k prototype >= 1 in {null_hits} of 2000 draws")
assert 8 <= null_hits <= 36
say("assert passed: 8 <= draws <= 36")
say(f"W: trace {np.trace(W):.3f}, largest eigenvalue {np.linalg.eigvalsh(W)[-1]:.3f}, from {len(groups)} lines")

# A1 profile in PC coordinates
say("\n== A1 profile in PC coordinates ==")
total_variance = pcs.var(axis=0, ddof=1)
diagonals = np.array([np.diag(np.cov(pcs[groups[line]].T)) for line in LINES])
median_within, median_share = np.median(diagonals, axis=0), np.median(diagonals / total_variance, axis=0)
say("PC | median within-line variance | median share of the PC's total variance | total variance")
for j in range(P):
    say(f"PC{j + 1} | {median_within[j]:.3f} | {median_share[j]:.3f} | {total_variance[j]:.3f}")
off = []
for line in LINES:
    s = np.cov(pcs[groups[line]].T)
    off.append(float(1 - np.sum(np.diag(s) ** 2) / np.sum(s ** 2)))
    out_lines[line]["A1"] = {"within_variance": np.diag(s).tolist(), "offdiagonal_share": off[-1]}
say(f"share of off-diagonal energy in S: {spread(off)}")

# A2 every line whitened by W without that line
say("\n== A2 whitened by W without the line ==")


def whitened(line, points=pcs, p=parts, t=total, d=dof):
    x = points[groups[line]]
    return (x - x.mean(axis=0)) @ inverse_root(without(p, t, d, line))


t1_a2, _, k_prototype_a2 = run_step("A2", whitened)

# A3 sequencing depth, recovered from CPM as in depth_confound_20261107/real.py
say("\n== A3 sequencing depth ==")
cpm = ad.read_h5ad(ROOT / "local_data/choir/kinker_2020/kinker_2020_choir_cells_cpm.h5ad")
assert (cpm.obs.index == metadata.index).all()
x = sparse.csr_matrix(cpm.X, dtype=np.float64)
lib = 1e6 / np.minimum.reduceat(x.data, x.indptr[:-1])
counts = sparse.diags(lib / 1e6) @ x
assert np.abs(counts.data - np.rint(counts.data)).max() < 1e-6
counts.data = np.rint(counts.data)
log_depth = np.log(np.asarray(counts.sum(axis=1)).ravel())  # depth = total recovered counts per cell
del cpm, x, counts
say(f"log depth: median {np.median(log_depth):.3f}, sd {log_depth.std():.3f}, cells {len(log_depth)}")
residual = np.empty_like(pcs)
for line, index in groups.items():
    design = np.column_stack([np.ones(len(index)), log_depth[index]])
    beta, *_ = np.linalg.lstsq(design, pcs[index], rcond=None)
    residual[index] = pcs[index] - design @ beta
explained, correlation = [], []
for line in LINES:
    index = groups[line]
    explained.append(float(1 - np.trace(np.cov(residual[index].T)) / np.trace(np.cov(pcs[index].T))))
    first = np.linalg.eigh(np.cov(pcs[index].T))[1][:, -1]
    correlation.append(float(np.corrcoef(pcs[index] @ first, log_depth[index])[0, 1]))
    out_lines[line]["A3"] = {"depth_share_of_trace": explained[-1], "first_score_corr_log_depth": correlation[-1]}
say(f"share of the within-line variance (trace of S) explained by log depth: {spread(explained)}")
say(f"|correlation| of the first principal score with log depth: {spread(np.abs(correlation))}")
t1_a3, _, _ = run_step("A3 depth removed", lambda line: residual[groups[line]])
r_parts, r_total, r_dof = pooled(residual, groups)
run_step("A3 depth removed, whitened", lambda line: whitened(line, residual, r_parts, r_total, r_dof))

# A4 pools
say("\n== A4 pools ==")
pools = metadata.groupby("Cell_line").agg(pool_id=("pool_id", "nunique"), source_library=("source_library", "nunique"))
say(f"lines (all {len(pools)}) with more than one pool_id: {int((pools.pool_id > 1).sum())}; "
    f"with more than one source_library: {int((pools.source_library > 1).sum())}")
say(f"of the 175 lines: more than one pool_id {int((pools.loc[LINES].pool_id > 1).sum())}; "
    f"more than one source_library {int((pools.loc[LINES].source_library > 1).sum())}")
pool_share = []
for line in LINES:
    index = groups[line]
    if pools.loc[line, "pool_id"] == 1 and pools.loc[line, "source_library"] == 1:
        continue
    x = pcs[index]
    means = pd.DataFrame(x).groupby(metadata["source_library"].to_numpy()[index]).transform("mean").to_numpy()
    pool_share.append(float(np.sum((means - x.mean(axis=0)) ** 2) / np.sum((x - x.mean(axis=0)) ** 2)))
    out_lines[line]["A4"] = {"source_libraries": int(pools.loc[line, "source_library"]), "pool_share": pool_share[-1]}
say(f"share of the within-line variance explained by the source_library means ({len(pool_share)} lines): {spread(pool_share)}")

# A5 what remains after A2
say("\n== A5 after A2 ==")
q = np.array([null_quantile(len(groups[line]), P) for line in LINES])
say(f"k prototype 0: {int((k_prototype_a2 == 0).sum())}, 1: {int((k_prototype_a2 == 1).sum())}, "
    f"2 and more: {int((k_prototype_a2 >= 2).sum())} (total {len(LINES)})")
say(f"T1 above q: {int((t1_a2 > q).sum())} of {len(LINES)}; T1 / q {spread(t1_a2 / q)}; q {spread(q)}")
for line, value in zip(LINES, q):
    out_lines[line]["q"] = float(value)

say("\n== predictions ==")
say(f"PA1 median T1 after A2 below 5: {np.median(t1_a2):.3f} -> {'held' if np.median(t1_a2) < 5 else 'not held'}")
hits = int((k_prototype_a2 >= 1).sum())
say(f"PA2 k prototype >= 1 after A2 in more than half the lines: {hits} of {len(LINES)} -> "
    f"{'held' if hits > len(LINES) / 2 else 'not held'}")
say(f"PA3 median T1 after removing the depth fit above 10: {np.median(t1_a3):.3f} -> "
    f"{'held' if np.median(t1_a3) > 10 else 'not held'}")
say(f"\nrun time {perf_counter() - START:.1f} s")
(HERE / "a_lines.txt").write_text("\n".join(report) + "\n")
(HERE / "a_lines.json").write_text(json.dumps({"null_draws_with_k_prototype": int(null_hits), "lines": out_lines}, indent=1))

def inverse_root(matrix):
    values, vectors = np.linalg.eigh(matrix)
    return vectors @ np.diag(values ** -0.5) @ vectors.T


def t1(cells):
    values = np.linalg.eigvalsh(np.cov(cells.T))
    return values[-1] / np.median(values)


before, after = [], []
for line, index in groups.items():
    if len(index) < 100:
        continue
    w = (total_scatter - scatter[line]) / (total_df - (len(index) - 1))

    before.append(t1(cells))
    after.append(t1(cells @ inverse_root(w)))

def rows_under(node):
    nodes = order[pre[node] : pre[node] + sub[node]]
    return leaf_row[nodes[nodes < n_cells]]


def pooled(groups):
    """Pooled covariance: sum of (n - 1) S over the groups divided by the sum of (n - 1)."""
    total = sum((len(g) - 1) * np.cov(pcs[g].T) for g in groups if len(g) > 1)
    return total / sum(len(g) - 1 for g in groups)


def inverse_root(c):
    values, vectors = np.linalg.eigh(c)
    return vectors @ np.diag(values**-0.5) @ vectors.T


def ks(points):
    values = np.linalg.eigvalsh(np.cov(points.T))[::-1]
    return float(values[0] / np.median(values)), len(invariant_directions(points)), len(directions_with_margin(points))


def med(x):
    x = np.asarray(x, dtype=float)
    return f"median {np.median(x):.3g} (min {x.min():.3g}, max {x.max():.3g})" if len(x) else "none"


def dist(x):
    return dict(sorted(Counter(int(v) for v in x).items()))

        top[node] = node
members = pd.Series(leaf_row, index=top[:n]).groupby(level=0).apply(list)
members = members[(members.index >= 0) & (members.map(len) >= 5)]
scatter_tree, df_tree = np.zeros((30, 30)), 0
for rows in members:
    cells = pcs[rows] - pcs[rows].mean(axis=0)
    scatter_tree += cells.T @ cells
    df_tree += len(rows) - 1
w_tree = scatter_tree / df_tree
groups = metadata.groupby("Cell_line").indices
w = sum((len(i) - 1) * np.cov(pcs[i].T) for i in groups.values()) / sum(len(i) - 1 for i in groups.values())


def inverse_root(matrix):
    values, vectors = np.linalg.eigh(matrix)
    return vectors @ np.diag(values ** -0.5) @ vectors.T


ratio = np.linalg.eigvalsh(inverse_root(w) @ w_tree @ inverse_root(w))
print(f"subtrees {len(members)}; cells covered {int(members.map(len).sum())}; eigenvalues of W^-1/2 W_tree(50) W^-1/2: "
      f"smallest {ratio.min():.3f}, median {np.median(ratio):.3f}, largest {ratio.max():.3f}")
t1 = []
for index in groups.values():
    if len(index) >= 100:
        values = np.linalg.eigvalsh(np.cov(((pcs[index] - pcs[index].mean(axis=0)) @ inverse_root(w_tree)).T))
        t1.append(values[-1] / np.median(values))
print(f"175 lines whitened by W_tree(50): T1 median {np.median(t1):.1f} (min {min(t1):.2f}, max {max(t1):.0f})")
# which direction is the one where the small subtrees hold least of the within-line variance?
values, vectors = np.linalg.eigh(inverse_root(w) @ w_tree @ inverse_root(w))
direction = inverse_root(w) @ vectors[:, 0]
print("direction with the smallest ratio, largest PC loadings (PC, weight):",
      [(int(j) + 1, round(float(direction[j] / np.abs(direction).max()), 2)) for j in np.argsort(-np.abs(direction))[:5]])
