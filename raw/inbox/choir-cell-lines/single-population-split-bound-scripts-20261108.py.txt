# ===== protocol.md =====
# Protocol: the single-population bound on a split (frozen 2026-11-08)

## Question (user-confirmed)

Infer the mathematical rule for the allowed overlap against noise splits the tree picks, instead of an
empirical calibration, keeping the rule from tightening as the number of cells grows.

## Derivation to check

Notation: a split of n cells into shares pi and 1 - pi; D = Mahalanobis distance of the two side means under the
pooled within-sides covariance; rho = pi (1 - pi) D^2 = between / within variance along the split's Fisher
discriminant; Hotelling T = n rho.

- D1. For a fixed direction u and share pi, the threshold cut on u at the pi-quantile has the largest
  between/within ratio along u of all splits with that share (it maximises the gap of the side means while the
  total variance along u is fixed). Fisher's D^2 is the maximum over u. So the largest rho over all 2^(n-1) - 1
  splits is reached by a flat cut.
- D2. One Gaussian population, any covariance: every flat cut at share pi has rho = g(pi) = r / (1 - r),
  r = phi(Phi^-1(pi))^2 / (pi (1 - pi)). Balanced: r = 2/pi, g = 1.752. By D1, no split of one Gaussian
  population exceeds g(pi).
- D3. One population whose projections are unimodal: rho <= 3 (Khintchine representation X = mode + U Y; proof
  for cuts at the mode, numerical check for the rest). Reached by the uniform distribution at its median.
- D4. Two Gaussian groups at separation delta > 0: a flat cut through the gap exceeds g(pi), so every delta > 0
  is detectable as n grows. If the split's labels are the true groups, rho = pi (1 - pi) delta^2, which exceeds
  g(pi) only if delta > delta_0(pi) = sqrt(g(pi) / (pi (1 - pi))); their overlap (Bayes error) may then be up to
  tau_0(pi). Balanced: delta_0 = 2.647, tau_0 = 9.3%.
- D5. The overlap fraction does not decide: a flat cut of one population has zero overlap and is separable.
- D6. Finite n, on the k invariant directions of the subtree (noise band, as in production): at the null
  boundary (a fixed flat cut of one Gaussian population), T is about noncentral chi2 with k degrees of freedom
  and noncentrality n g(pi). The tree may pick any split; by D1 the worst picks are flat cuts, of which there are
  M_k(n) = sum_{i=0}^{k} binom(n-1, i) (Cover 1965). Rule:
  p = min{1, M_k(n) P(chi2_k(n g(pi_hat)) >= T)}, and p = 1 when k = 0 (always when n <= p_features + 1).
  Unimodal variant: g replaced by 3. The required rho falls toward g(pi) as n grows (excess about
  sqrt(g log n / n)), so the allowed overlap rises toward tau_0(pi) instead of shrinking.

## Part A checks (derive.py)

- A0. Bayes error of two unit Gaussians with shares pi, 1 - pi at separation delta: formula
  pi Phi(c - delta/2) + (1 - pi) Phi(-c - delta/2), c = ln((1 - pi)/pi) / delta, against 2 x 10^6 simulated cells
  at pi = 0.1 and 0.5; also the `allowed_overlap` of `rule_out_overlap_20261108` (suspected swapped terms).
- A1. 2 x 10^6 Gaussian draws cut at the pi-quantile, pi in {0.5, 0.2, 0.05}: rho within 1% of g(pi).
- A2. n = 12 points in 2 dimensions, 20 seeds: over all 2^11 - 1 splits, the split with the largest rho, overall
  and for each smaller-side size, is linearly separable.
- A3. Largest rho over cuts for uniform, Gaussian, Laplace, logistic, t5, exponential and lognormal(1)
  (2 x 10^6 draws), and over 20,000 random mixtures of uniforms anchored at the mode: at most 3 (+1%).
- A4. Fixed median cut, 1 dimension, n = 1,000, 4,000 replicates: share of P(chi2_1(n g) >= T) <= 0.01 is at
  most 0.01 plus Monte Carlo error; mean and variance of T against n g + 1 and 4 n g + 2 (derived delta-method
  variance (4a^2 - 6a^4)/(1 - a^2)^4 n = 6.58 n, a^2 = 2/pi).
- A5. Tree-picked splits: Ward's top split on the same 30 coordinates; k from the noise band
  (`split_count_rule_out.invariant_directions`, same rule as the E2 harness). Populations: Gaussian gradient
  (variance 9 on one axis), flat gradient (uniform, variance 9 on one axis), two unit Gaussian groups at
  delta in {2, 3, 4} along one axis with shares 0.5 and 0.2. n in {64, 128, 256, 512, 1024, 2048, 4096};
  200 replicates for the gradients, 100 for the groups. Predictions: Gaussian gradient kept (p <= 0.01) at most
  1% at every n under both bounds; flat gradient kept at most 1% under the unimodal bound and rising with n
  under the Gaussian bound (a flat gradient is not Gaussian; a limit of D2, stated in advance); two groups:
  kept does not fall with n under the Gaussian bound; under the unimodal bound groups at delta <= 2 (one-mode
  mixtures) stay out. The separable share (old rule) is reported for contrast.
- A6. Allowed overlap table: smallest rho with p <= 0.01 for k = 1, n in {100, 1,000, 10,000, 48,879},
  pi in {0.5, 0.2, 0.1}, both bounds, and the Bayes error of true-label groups at that rho.

# ===== deviations.md =====
# Deviations from protocol.md

- A3 failed: the random search over unimodal mixtures found rho = 7.84 (> 3.03). The case is a unimodal
  population with a thin, long, flat tail, cut inside the tail. Construction added to derive.py: a flat tail
  U(-L, 0) of mass eps cut at a L from the mode gives rho -> 3 (1 - a)(1 + a)^2 / ((1 - a)^3 + 4 a^3) = 8 at
  a = 1/3 (computed 7.998). D3 holds only for cuts at the mode. The unimodal_cut arm keeps the frozen bound 3
  and is reported as the at-the-mode bound, not as an upper bound for all unimodal populations. The A3 asserts
  were replaced by reporting.
- A5 prediction "Gaussian gradient kept at most 1% at every n" missed at 64 cells: 3 of 200 (1.5%, Monte Carlo
  sd 0.7%); 0 of 200 at every larger n.

"""protocol.md D2-D6: the largest split one population allows, and the p-value that a split goes beyond it."""

import numpy as np
from scipy.special import gammaln, logsumexp
from scipy.stats import ncx2, norm

UNIMODAL_BOUND = 3.0


def gaussian_bound(pi):
    """g(pi): between/within ratio of a flat cut of one Gaussian population into shares pi and 1 - pi."""
    r = norm.pdf(norm.ppf(pi)) ** 2 / (pi * (1 - pi))
    return r / (1 - r)


def unimodal_bound(pi):
    return UNIMODAL_BOUND


def bayes_error(pi, delta):
    """Error of the best rule for two unit Gaussians at separation delta with shares pi and 1 - pi."""
    c = np.log((1 - pi) / pi) / delta  # the boundary moves toward the smaller group
    return float(pi * norm.cdf(c - delta / 2) + (1 - pi) * norm.cdf(-c - delta / 2))


def fisher_ratio(points, in_left):
    """rho = pi (1 - pi) D^2; D = Mahalanobis distance of the side means, pooled within covariance (n - 2)."""
    left, right = points[in_left], points[~in_left]
    n, pi = len(points), len(left) / len(points)
    centered = np.vstack([left - left.mean(0), right - right.mean(0)])
    d = left.mean(0) - right.mean(0)
    return float(pi * (1 - pi) * d @ np.linalg.solve(centered.T @ centered / (n - 2), d))


def log_flat_cuts(n, k):
    """log sum_{i<=k} binom(n-1, i): splits of n points in general position by a flat cut in k dimensions."""
    i = np.arange(0, min(k, n - 1) + 1)
    return float(logsumexp(gammaln(n) - gammaln(i + 1) - gammaln(n - i)))


def cut_bound_p(points, in_left, bound):
    """(p, rho): count of flat cuts x P(noncentral chi2_k(n bound(pi)) >= n rho); p = 1 without directions."""
    n, k = points.shape
    if k == 0:
        return 1.0, float("nan")
    rho = fisher_ratio(points, in_left)
    log_p = log_flat_cuts(n, k) + ncx2.logsf(n * rho, k, n * bound(float(in_left.mean())))
    return float(np.exp(min(0.0, log_p))), rho


if __name__ == "__main__":
    assert abs(gaussian_bound(0.5) - (2 / np.pi) / (1 - 2 / np.pi)) < 1e-12
    assert abs(bayes_error(0.5, 2.0) - norm.cdf(-1.0)) < 1e-12
    assert bayes_error(0.1, 2.4) < 0.1  # never above the smaller share
    assert abs(np.exp(log_flat_cuts(5, 1)) - 5) < 1e-9  # 4 thresholds on a line + the empty cut
    rng = np.random.default_rng(0)
    x = np.r_[rng.standard_normal((20000, 3)) + [2, 0, 0], rng.standard_normal((20000, 3)) - [2, 0, 0]]
    side = np.r_[np.ones(20000, bool), np.zeros(20000, bool)]
    assert abs(fisher_ratio(x, side) - 4.0) < 0.1, fisher_ratio(x, side)  # 0.25 * 4^2
    assert np.isfinite(ncx2.logsf(5000.0, 1, 3000.0)) and ncx2.logsf(5000.0, 1, 3000.0) < -100
    print("self-check passed", round(gaussian_bound(0.5), 4), round(gaussian_bound(0.2), 4),
          round(fisher_ratio(x, side), 4), float(ncx2.logsf(5000.0, 1, 3000.0)))

def cut_ratios(sorted_x):
    """rho of every threshold cut of a sorted 1D sample (left = the first i values)."""
    n = len(sorted_x)
    i = np.arange(1, n)
    s1, s2 = np.cumsum(sorted_x)[:-1], np.cumsum(sorted_x**2)[:-1]
    t1, t2 = sorted_x.sum(), (sorted_x**2).sum()
    between = (s1 / i - (t1 - s1) / (n - i)) ** 2 * i * (n - i) / n**2
    total = t2 / n - (t1 / n) ** 2
    return i / n, between / (total - between)


def a0_bayes_error():
    say("A0 Bayes error, 2e6 cells: pi, delta, simulated, formula, overlap_rule.allowed_overlap")
    rng = np.random.default_rng(10)
    for pi in (0.1, 0.5):
        delta = np.sqrt(2 * np.log(2) / (pi * (1 - pi)))  # the separation allowed_overlap uses
        a = rng.random(2_000_000) < pi
        x = rng.standard_normal(len(a)) + np.where(a, delta / 2, -delta / 2)
        c = np.log((1 - pi) / pi) / delta
        simulated = np.mean((x > c) != a)
        say("  ", pi, round(delta, 3), round(simulated, 4), round(bayes_error(pi, delta), 4),
            round(allowed_overlap(pi), 4))


def a1_gaussian_cut():
    say("A1 flat cut of 2e6 Gaussian draws: pi, rho, g(pi)")
    x = np.sort(np.random.default_rng(11).standard_normal(2_000_000))
    share, rho = cut_ratios(x)
    for pi in (0.5, 0.2, 0.05):
        value = rho[np.searchsorted(share, pi)]
        say("  ", pi, round(value, 4), round(gaussian_bound(pi), 4))
        assert abs(value / gaussian_bound(pi) - 1) < 0.01


def a2_best_split_is_flat():
    hits, total = 0, 0
    for seed in range(20):
        x = np.random.default_rng(seed).standard_normal((12, 2))
        best = {}
        for mask in itertools.product((False, True), repeat=11):
            side = np.r_[True, mask]
            if side.all():
                continue
            m = int(min(side.sum(), (~side).sum()))
            rho = fisher_ratio(x, side)
            if rho > best.get(m, (-1, None))[0]:
                best[m] = (rho, side)
        overall = max(best.values(), key=lambda b: b[0])
        for rho, side in [*best.values(), overall]:
            total += 1
            hits += is_separable(x, side)
    say(f"A2 largest-rho split linearly separable (per smaller-side size and overall): {hits}/{total}")
    assert hits == total


def uniform_mixture_ratios(lo, hi, w, cuts):
    """rho at each cut for a mixture of uniforms on [lo_j, hi_j] with weights w_j (one mixture)."""
    width = hi - lo

    def below(t):
        c = np.clip(t[:, None], lo, hi)
        return ((c - lo) / width @ w, (c**2 - lo**2) / (2 * width) @ w, (c**3 - lo**3) / (3 * width) @ w)

    m0, m1, m2 = below(cuts)
    t1, t2 = (hi**2 - lo**2) / (2 * width) @ w, (hi**3 - lo**3) / (3 * width) @ w
    ok = (m0 > 1e-9) & (m0 < 1 - 1e-9)
    mean_l, mean_r = m1[ok] / m0[ok], (t1 - m1[ok]) / (1 - m0[ok])
    between = m0[ok] * (1 - m0[ok]) * (mean_r - mean_l) ** 2
    return between / (t2 - t1**2 - between)


def a3_unimodal():
    say("A3 largest rho over cuts (2e6 draws): family, rho, at pi")
    rng = np.random.default_rng(12)
    families = {"uniform": rng.random, "gaussian": rng.standard_normal, "laplace": lambda n: rng.laplace(size=n),
                "logistic": lambda n: rng.logistic(size=n), "t5": lambda n: rng.standard_t(5, n),
                "exponential": lambda n: rng.exponential(size=n), "lognormal1": lambda n: rng.lognormal(0, 1, n)}
    for name, draw in families.items():
        share, rho = cut_ratios(np.sort(draw(2_000_000)))
        j = int(np.argmax(rho))
        say("  ", name, round(rho[j], 4), round(share[j], 4))
    best = 0.0
    for _ in range(20000):
        m = rng.integers(1, 7)
        ends = np.where(rng.random(m) < 0.5, -1, 1) * np.exp(rng.normal(0, 1.5, m))
        lo, hi = np.minimum(ends, 0.0), np.maximum(ends, 0.0)  # Khintchine: uniforms anchored at the mode 0
        w = rng.dirichlet(np.ones(m))
        cuts = np.r_[np.linspace(lo.min(), hi.max(), 1500), ends, 0.0]
        best = max(best, float(uniform_mixture_ratios(lo, hi, w, np.sort(cuts)).max()))
    say(f"A3 random unimodal mixtures (20,000): largest rho {best:.4f} (protocol expected <= 3.03)")
    # added after the search exceeded 3 (deviations.md): a thin flat tail U(-L, 0) of mass eps cut at a L from the
    # mode gives rho -> 3 (1 - a)(1 + a)^2 / ((1 - a)^3 + 4 a^3), which is 8 at a = 1/3
    tail = uniform_mixture_ratios(np.array([0.0, -1e4]), np.array([1.0, 0.0]), np.array([1 - 1e-4, 1e-4]),
                                  np.array([-1e4 / 3]))
    say(f"A3 thin flat tail cut at a third of its length: rho {tail[0]:.4f} (construction: 8)")


def a4_fixed_cut():
    n, reps = 1000, 4000
    g = gaussian_bound(0.5)
    rng = np.random.default_rng(13)
    t = np.empty(reps)
    for r in range(reps):
        z = rng.standard_normal(n)
        t[r] = n * fisher_ratio(z[:, None], z < np.median(z))
    p = ncx2.sf(t, 1, n * g)
    a2 = 2 / np.pi
    say(f"A4 fixed median cut, n = {n}: share p <= 0.01 = {np.mean(p <= 0.01):.4f} (MC sd 0.0016); mean T "
        f"{t.mean():.1f} vs n g + 1 = {n * g + 1:.1f}; var T {t.var():.0f} vs 4 n g + 2 = {4 * n * g + 2:.0f}, "
        f"delta-method {(4 * a2 - 6 * a2**2) / (1 - a2) ** 4 * n:.0f}")


def population(name, n, rng):
    x = rng.standard_normal((n, P))
    if name == "gaussian_gradient":
        x[:, 0] *= 3.0
    elif name == "flat_gradient":
        x[:, 0] = rng.uniform(-3 * np.sqrt(3), 3 * np.sqrt(3), n)
    else:  # groups_<delta>_<share>
        _, delta, share = name.split("_")
        x[: int(round(float(share) * n)), 0] += float(delta)
    return x


def a5_task(job):
    name, n, rep = job
    with threadpool_limits(limits=1):
        rng = np.random.default_rng([zlib.crc32(name.encode()), n, rep])
        x = population(name, n, rng)
        side = fcluster(linkage(x, "ward"), 2, "maxclust") == 1
        directions = invariant_directions(x)
        row = {"population": name, "n": n, "rep": rep, "k": len(directions), "pi": float(side.mean())}
        if len(directions) == 0:
            return {**row, "rho": np.nan, "p_gaussian": 1.0, "p_unimodal": 1.0, "p_gaussian_no_count": 1.0,
                    "separable": False}
        z = x @ directions.T
        p_g, rho = cut_bound_p(z, side, gaussian_bound)
        p_u, _ = cut_bound_p(z, side, unimodal_bound)
        no_count = float(np.exp(min(0.0, ncx2.logsf(n * rho, len(directions), n * gaussian_bound(row["pi"])))))
        return {**row, "rho": rho, "g": gaussian_bound(row["pi"]), "p_gaussian": p_g, "p_unimodal": p_u,
                "p_gaussian_no_count": no_count, "separable": bool(is_separable(z, side))}


def a5_jobs():
    groups = [f"groups_{d}_{s}" for d in (2, 3, 4) for s in (0.5, 0.2)]
    jobs = [(name, n, r) for name in ("gaussian_gradient", "flat_gradient") for n in NS for r in range(200)]
    return jobs + [(name, n, r) for name in groups for n in NS for r in range(100)]


def a5_summary(rows):
    t = pd.DataFrame(rows)
    t["kept_gaussian"] = t.p_gaussian <= 0.01
    t["kept_unimodal"] = t.p_unimodal <= 0.01
    t["kept_no_count"] = t.p_gaussian_no_count <= 0.01
    s = t.groupby(["population", "n"]).agg(
        runs=("k", "size"), with_direction=("k", lambda k: (k > 0).mean()), median_pi=("pi", "median"),
        median_rho=("rho", "median"), median_g=("g", "median"), kept_gaussian=("kept_gaussian", "mean"),
        kept_unimodal=("kept_unimodal", "mean"), kept_no_count=("kept_no_count", "mean"),
        separable=("separable", "mean"))
    return s.round(3).to_string()


def a6_allowed_overlap():
    say("A6 smallest rho with p <= 0.01 (k = 1) and the overlap of true-label groups at that rho")
    rows = []
    for bound_name, bound in (("gaussian", gaussian_bound), ("unimodal", unimodal_bound)):
        for pi in (0.5, 0.2, 0.1):
            for n in (100, 1000, 10000, 48879):
                b = bound(pi)
                rho = brentq(lambda v: log_flat_cuts(n, 1) + ncx2.logsf(n * v, 1, n * b) - np.log(0.01), b, b + 100)
                delta = np.sqrt(rho / (pi * (1 - pi)))
                rows.append({"bound": bound_name, "pi": pi, "n": n, "limit_rho": round(b, 3), "rho_needed": round(rho, 3),
                             "delta_true_labels": round(delta, 3), "allowed_overlap": round(bayes_error(pi, delta), 4)})
            delta0 = np.sqrt(b / (pi * (1 - pi)))
            rows.append({"bound": bound_name, "pi": pi, "n": "limit", "limit_rho": round(b, 3), "rho_needed": round(b, 3),
                         "delta_true_labels": round(delta0, 3), "allowed_overlap": round(bayes_error(pi, delta0), 4)})
    say(pd.DataFrame(rows).to_string(index=False))

    k, basis = old.counting.invariant_directions(points)
    z = points @ basis.T
    m, _ = old.distances.margins(old.distances.whiten(points, own_a), own_a)

    return {"n_parent": n, "n_small": int(min(own_a.sum(), (~own_a).sum())), "k": k, "pi": pi,
            "separable": bool(k >= 1 and old.counting.separable(z, own_a)),
            "tie_share": float(np.mean(np.abs(m) < old.TIE_MARGIN)), "misplaced": misplaced,
            "low_overlap_fixed": bool(k >= 1 and misplaced <= allowed_overlap_fixed(pi)),
            "rho": rho, "g": gaussian_bound(pi), "p_gaussian": p_gaussian, "p_unimodal": p_unimodal}


def swapped(records, *, model, **kwargs):
    ids, summaries, labels, decisions, inadmissible = old.ORIGINAL(records, model=model, **kwargs)
    arm, cache = old.STATE["arm"], old.STATE["cache"]
    tree = sys._getframe(1).f_locals["tree"]
    by_parent = {r.parent: r for r in records}
    if "rows" not in cache:
        cache["rows"] = old.precompute_descendants(tree, {label: i for i, label in enumerate(old.STATE["data"].index)})
        cache["matrix"] = old.STATE["data"].to_numpy(dtype=np.float64)
    rows, matrix = cache["rows"], cache["matrix"]
    out = ([], [], [], [], list(inadmissible))
    for pid, summary, label, decision in zip(ids, summaries, labels, decisions, strict=True):
        record = by_parent[pid]
        if decision is None:
            for column, value in zip(out[:4], (pid, summary, label, decision), strict=True):
                column.append(value)
            continue
        key = ("split", str(pid))
        if key not in cache:
            left, right = sorted(rows[record.left]), sorted(rows[record.right])
            cache[key] = split_facts(matrix[left + right], np.r_[np.ones(len(left), bool), np.zeros(len(right), bool)])
            old.count("n_matches_record", cache[key]["n_parent"] == int(record.n_parent))
        s = cache[key]
        stat, df = float(record.stat), float(record.degrees_of_freedom)
        if arm in ("rule_out", "rule_out_overlap_fixed"):
            real = (s["separable"] if arm == "rule_out" else s["low_overlap_fixed"]) and s["tie_share"] <= old.MAX_TIE_SHARE
            p = old.adjusted(stat, df, old.counting.log_all_splits(s["n_parent"])) if real else 1.0
        else:
            real = None
            p = s["p_gaussian"] if arm == "gaussian_cut" else s["p_unimodal"]
        old.STATE["splits"].append({"node": str(pid), **s, "stat": stat, "df": df, "real": real, "p": p,
                                    "c_today": float(decision.c_hat)})
        out[0].append(pid)
        out[1].append((stat, df, p))
        out[2].append(f"{label}:{arm}")
        out[3].append(old.replace(decision, c_hat=1.0, p_value=p))
    return out


pipeline.compute_inflation_adjusted_sibling_tests = swapped


def task(job):
    return old.task(job)


def main(workers):
    paths = {name: HERE / f"{name}.jsonl" for name in ("datasets", "splits", "checks")}
    done = set()
    if paths["checks"].exists():
        done = {(r["model"], r["replicate"]) for r in map(json.loads, paths["checks"].read_text().splitlines())}
    todo = [j for j in old.jobs() if j not in done]
    files = {name: path.open("a", buffering=1) for name, path in paths.items()}
    with Pool(workers) as pool:
        for i, (datasets, splits, checks) in enumerate(pool.imap_unordered(task, todo), 1):
            files["splits"].write("".join(json.dumps(r, default=float) + "\n" for r in splits))
            files["datasets"].write("".join(json.dumps(r) + "\n" for r in datasets))
            files["checks"].write(json.dumps(checks) + "\n")
            if i % 25 == 0:
                print(f"{i}/{len(todo)}", flush=True)


if __name__ == "__main__":
    if sys.argv[1] == "check":
        for job in (("high", 0), ("signal_density_variation", 0)):
            t = perf_counter()
            datasets, splits, checks = task(job)
            print(job, f"{perf_counter() - t:.1f}s", checks)
            for d in datasets:
                print(d["tree"], d["arm"], d["found_clusters"], round(d["ari"], 3), round(d["sec"], 1))
            for s in splits:
                if s["arm"] == "gaussian_cut" and s["n_parent"] >= 100 and s["tree"] == "markov_ward":
                    print({k: (round(v, 3) if isinstance(v, float) else v) for k, v in s.items()
                           if k in ("n_parent", "k", "pi", "misplaced", "rho", "g", "p_gaussian", "p_unimodal")})
    else:
        main(int(sys.argv[1]))
