# Sprs applied to every refused split whose best flat cut passes, then the fit rerun (CHOIR cell lines)

Run on 2026-11-11, Europe/Vienna. Local folder: `results/analyses/spr_refit_20261111` (git-ignored); protocol
(frozen before the run), the recorded open choices (`deviations.md`), scripts, self-check output, summaries and
metrics are in `spr-refit-scripts-20261111.py.txt`. Trial; no gate, default or production file was changed. The
harness (part 1) and the spr pass (part 2) were written separately in parallel and each was checked with a
separate script before the refit.

## Setup

48,879 CHOIR cells, 30 PCs, `tbs_family_diffusion_markov_ward` with `sibling_best_flat_cut_reference=True`,
sibling alpha 0.01, edge alpha 0.001, registry parameters. The harness hands the runner a `PosetTree` built from
a parent array (it replaces `build_tree` in the runner's namespace at run time, no distances, no linkage, merge
heights by merge order); branch lengths are refit by NNLS on the given topology.

Replay check: the unchanged saved tree through the harness gives 138 clusters, 1,838 tested splits and an
assignment with ARI 1.0 against the saved baseline assignment; every recorded split equals the earlier fit
(same reason, k, p, BH flag). One run takes about 110 s.

## The spr pass

Top-down over the tree. A node is a candidate when it has 15 cells or more, 1 to 10 invariant directions, a
sibling reference p above 0.01 for its own split, and a best flat cut with reference p at or below 0.01. The
smaller side of its best flat cut is made a node by sprs and regrafted on the edge above the candidate; both
children are then evaluated on the cells they hold.

| --- | ---: |
| more than 10 invariant directions (left as they are) | 194 |
| tree split already at p <= 0.01 | 42 |

- 4,371 sprs in all, 2,831 of them move a single leaf. Sprs per candidate: 1 to 223, median about 17; 14
  candidates needed 1, 13 needed 2, 8 needed 3.
- 108 candidates hold several cell lines, 58 one line. In 29 the side and the rest share no line.
- 110 candidates are nodes of the unchanged tree; 90 of them were tested and refused splits of the baseline fit.
- Self-check: on N96475, N96842 and N96808 the array implementation and `subtree_prune_regraft` on a `PosetTree`
  give equal `to_splits`, with 2 sprs each and `rho` 7.651, 10.305 and 13.693; 60 sampled nodes equal
  `measures.jsonl`; 166 of 166 candidates have the best flat cut's `rho` and p <= 0.01 in the output tree.
- Separate check of the output tree (`verify_tree.py`): a rooted binary tree on the same leaves; 8,760 of
  97,757 leaf sets changed; all 106 leaf sets with 1,000 leaves or more unchanged; for the 110 candidates that
  are nodes of the unchanged tree the leaf set is still a node, its split has the best flat cut's `rho` and the
  sibling reference p is <= 0.01 (110 of 110).

## Reading

- Inside the fit the sprs work as measured per split: the changed splits pass, BH accepts about half of them,
  and the number of accepted splits rises from 31 to 104.
- The gain is paid mostly in lines that become separated from other lines (merged only 91 to 51, lines without
  a cluster 75 to 41) and partly in lines that are divided (split only 9 to 47).
- The ARI stays low (0.34; ceiling 3 on the same tree had 0.83, the divisive trial 0.87), as expected before the
  run: the 194 nodes with more than 10 invariant directions, which include every node with 1,000 cells or more,
  are refused by the reference and the sprs do not reach them. The largest cluster keeps 36 lines.

## Limits

One dataset, one tree, one run; candidates are chosen by the same data that is then tested, and the reference
does not cover the choice of k or of the node; no null calibration of the complete procedure, so the 18
accepted splits inside single lines cannot be judged; the comparison of the array sprs with
`subtree_prune_regraft` was run move by move on three nodes only, the others are checked by their end state;
the two root edges take their ratio from the merge heights, which are by merge order in the harness (the replay
of the unchanged tree is exact all the same); splits with more than 10 invariant directions are not touched.
