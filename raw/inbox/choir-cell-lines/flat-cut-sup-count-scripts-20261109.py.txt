# ===== protocol.md =====
# Protocol: a tighter count than all flat cuts (frozen 2026-11-09, before check.py and apply.py)

User choice: derive a tighter count of the splits a Ward tree can pick, in place of the count of all flat cuts.

## Derivation

E1. Any split the tree picks, by any rule, has ratio at most the best flat cut's (D1 of
    `gaussian_cut_rule_20261108`). So p = P(sup over flat cuts of rho_hat >= rho_obs) is valid for Ward's split
    without counting cuts. The old rule bounds this sup by a union over all M_k(n) ~ n^k flat cuts, ignoring that
    neighbouring cuts share almost all cells.
E2. Whiten by the sample total covariance. For centred cells and the cut s_i = sign(v'x_i), the mean difference
    is d = m / (2 pi(1 - pi)) with m = (1/n) sum s_i x_i, so R^2 = pi(1 - pi) d'd = |m|^2 / (4 pi (1 - pi)) and
    rho = R^2 / (1 - R^2) (times (n - 2)/n for the pooled divisor). Over sign vectors, sup |m| = sup_v mean |v'x|
    (exchange of the two sups), the best direction's mean absolute deviation.
E3. Envelope argument (Hartigan 1978 for split criteria, Pollard 1982 for k-means): the sup sits, to first order,
    on the population's best cuts, so the cut's threshold and side shares add only O(1/n). Per direction,
    R_hat(v) = mean|t| / sd(t) with t = v'x; linearised, sqrt(n)(R_hat(v) - R*) is the mean of
    f(t) = |t| - c0 (t^2 + 1)/2, c0 = E|t|, var f = 1 - 3/pi (Gaussian) and 1/40 (uniform).
E4. Gaussian F: every direction is a best cut, so the sup runs over the sphere S^{k-1} (even field). Its
    covariance is (2/pi)(sqrt(1 - r^2) + r arcsin r - 1 - r^2/2) at r = v'w, giving the normalised second
    spectral moment lambda = (1 - 2/pi)/(1 - 3/pi) = 8.06. Ceiling 3: the uniform-on-the-sphere law of radius
    sqrt(3) in k = 3 has a uniform marginal in every direction (rho* = 3 everywhere); its lambda = 13.0 is
    used for every k (assumption; the ceiling alone does not fix lambda).
E5. The sup tail of an even unit-variance field on S^{k-1}: expected Euler characteristic with Lipschitz-Killing
    curvatures of the sphere of radius sqrt(lambda) (Adler-Taylor eq. 3.6.4), halved. Self-check: with
    lambda = 1 the formula equals P(chi_k >= u) (Adler-Taylor eq. 4.5.15).

## Checks

- C1 (null calibration): n in {100, 400, 1600}, 400 replicates; Gaussian k = 1, 2, 3 and ceiling 3 with the
  uniform (k = 1) and uniform on the sphere of radius sqrt(3) (k = 3). Sup over flat cuts: all thresholds;
  directions exact for k = 1, a grid (720 angles, 2,000 sphere points) refined by 5 Fisher re-sorts for k > 1.
  Endpoint: share of replicates with p <= 0.01 and <= 0.05, new rule and old flat-cut count. Prediction: new
  shares near alpha (within Monte Carlo error, 0.5% sd at 1%), old shares near 0.
- C2 (application, descriptive): ceiling 3, new rule, on the saved Markov/Ward ceiling3 tree: the rho each of the
  27 hidden-boundary top splits needs (old: 4.3-7.4) and how many pass; among the 1,838 tested splits, kept at
  p <= 0.01 by size, old vs new. No refit, BH or traversal; no production change.

# ===== deviations.md =====
# Deviations from protocol.md

- C1 failed for the frozen rule (u on the rho scale): share of single populations with p <= 0.01 was 2-4%
  (k = 1), 1-8% (k = 2) and 2-22% (k = 3); worst at 100 cells. rho is a convex function of R, so the linear
  approximation on the rho scale under-states the tail. Amended rule: u on the R scale (count_rule.sup_p; the
  frozen version is kept as sup_p_rho_scale). On the same draws the amended shares were 0-2.75% at 1%. Because
  the amendment was chosen after seeing those draws, C1 was rerun with fresh seeds and 1,000 replicates
  (check_seed1.jsonl); that run is the calibration result.
- Added after the endpoints: 4,000 one-dimensional Gaussian replicates at 400, 1,600 and 6,400 cells (fixed
  centre cut vs best cut, R scale): share above the 1% point 1.2-1.3% (fixed) and 1.3-1.5% (best); the best
  cut's mean excess 0.155, 0.079, 0.043 in sd units, shrinking like 1/sqrt(n).
- For k >= 4 the EC expansion swings between large positive and negative values at moderate u, and clipping at 0
  gave p = 0 at the ceiling itself (found in C2 before any result was used). Fix: p is the non-increasing upper
  envelope of the EC tail (its largest value at u or above), capped at 1; self-check added (p = 1 at the ceiling,
  p never smaller at a larger rho). The C1 shares at 1% and 5% are unchanged by the envelope.
- Added after the endpoints: null check at k = 6 and 9 (`check_highk.py`; Gaussian null with the Gaussian rule,
  product of uniforms with the ceiling-3 rule; best cut found by search, so a lower bound) and the null 99th
  percentile of the best cut against the rho each rule needs (`conservativeness.txt`).

# ===== count_rule.py =====
"""protocol.md: tail of the best flat cut of one population, in place of the count of all flat cuts.

Null: n cells from one population F whose best flat cut has ratio rho* = b. The tree's split has ratio at
most the best flat cut's (D1), so p = P(sup over flat cuts of rho_hat >= rho_obs). To first order the sup sits
on the population's best cuts (envelope argument; Hartigan 1978, Pollard 1982): in whitened coordinates
R_hat(v) = mean |v'x| / sd(v'x) at the cut through the centre, R^2 = rho/(1 + rho), and

    sqrt(n) (rho_hat(v) - b) ~ sigma_rho W(v),  W a unit-variance even Gaussian field on the sphere S^{k-1}.

Its sup tail is the expected Euler characteristic (Adler and Taylor):
    p = 1/2 sum_j L_j(S^{k-1} of radius sqrt(lambda)) rho_j(u),  u = sqrt(n) (rho_obs - b) / sigma_rho,
halved because W(v) = W(-v). Constants (derivations in protocol.md):
    Gaussian F:  b = 2/(pi - 2) = 1.752, sigma_rho^2 = a^2 (1 - 3/pi) = 6.582, lambda = (1 - 2/pi)/(1 - 3/pi) = 8.063
    ceiling 3 (uniform marginal at the cut): b = 3, sigma_rho^2 = a^2 / 40 = 19.2, lambda = 13.0 (isotropic law with
    uniform marginals, which exists for k = 3; used for every k, an assumption)
with a = d rho / d R = 2 R* / (1 - R*^2)^2.
"""

from functools import lru_cache

import numpy as np
from scipy.special import gammaln
from scipy.stats import norm

SHAPES = {
    "gaussian": {"b": 2 / (np.pi - 2), "R": np.sqrt(2 / np.pi), "var_R": 1 - 3 / np.pi, "lam": (1 - 2 / np.pi) / (1 - 3 / np.pi)},
    "ceiling3": {"b": 3.0, "R": np.sqrt(3) / 2, "var_R": 1 / 40, "lam": 13.0},
}


def sigma_rho(shape):
    s = SHAPES[shape]
    return 2 * s["R"] / (1 - s["R"] ** 2) ** 2 * np.sqrt(s["var_R"])


def log_sphere_area(m):
    """log s_m, the surface area of the unit sphere in R^m."""
    return np.log(2) + (m / 2) * np.log(np.pi) - gammaln(m / 2)


def lk_sphere(j, k, radius):
    """L_j of the sphere S^{k-1} of the given radius (Adler-Taylor eq. 3.6.4); 0 unless k - 1 - j is even."""
    if (k - 1 - j) % 2:
        return 0.0
    log_binom = gammaln(k) - gammaln(j + 1) - gammaln(k - j)
    return float(2 * np.exp(log_binom + log_sphere_area(k) - log_sphere_area(k - j) + j * np.log(radius)))


def ec_density(j, u):
    """rho_j(u) of a unit-variance Gaussian field: 1 - Phi(u) for j = 0, else (2 pi)^{-(j+1)/2} H_{j-1}(u) e^{-u^2/2}."""
    u = np.asarray(u, dtype=float)
    if j == 0:
        return norm.sf(u)
    h_prev, h = np.zeros_like(u), np.ones_like(u)  # probabilists' Hermite: H_{-1} unused, H_0 = 1
    for m in range(1, j):
        h_prev, h = h, u * h - (m - 1) * h_prev
    return (2 * np.pi) ** (-(j + 1) / 2) * h * np.exp(-u * u / 2)


def sup_tail(u, k, lam, even=True):
    """Expected-EC approximation of P(sup over S^{k-1} of a unit-variance field >= u)."""
    total = sum(lk_sphere(j, k, np.sqrt(lam)) * ec_density(j, u) for j in range(k))
    return total / 2 if even else total


U_GRID = np.arange(-10.0, 60.0, 0.002)


@lru_cache(maxsize=None)
def _envelope(k, lam):
    tail = np.asarray(sup_tail(U_GRID, k, lam), dtype=float)
    return np.maximum.accumulate(tail[::-1])[::-1]  # largest value at this u or above


def sup_tail_envelope(u, k, lam):
    """Non-increasing upper envelope of the even-field EC tail: its largest value at u or above (deviations.md).

    The EC expansion is accurate only for large u; for k >= 4 it swings between large positive and negative values
    at moderate u. The envelope keeps the large-u values and replaces the swings by their largest later value.
    """
    env = _envelope(int(k), float(lam))
    if u >= U_GRID[-1]:
        return float(max(sup_tail(u, k, lam), 0.0))
    return float(env[max(0, int(np.searchsorted(U_GRID, u, side="left")) - 1)])  # grid point at or below u: larger p


def sup_p_rho_scale(rho_obs, n, k, shape="ceiling3"):
    """Frozen version (protocol.md): linearised on the rho scale. Failed C1 (too many small p); kept for the record."""
    if k == 0 or not np.isfinite(rho_obs):
        return 1.0
    s = SHAPES[shape]
    u = np.sqrt(n) * (rho_obs - s["b"]) / sigma_rho(shape)
    return float(min(1.0, max(0.0, sup_tail(u, k, s["lam"]))))


def sup_p(rho_obs, n, k, shape="ceiling3"):
    """p that a split of n cells with ratio rho_obs on k invariant directions goes beyond one population's best cut.

    Amended (deviations.md): u on the scale of R = sqrt(rho'/(1 + rho')), rho' = rho n/(n - 2), where the
    linearisation of E3 holds; rho is a convex function of R and its tail is heavier than the linear one.
    """
    if k == 0 or not np.isfinite(rho_obs):
        return 1.0
    s = SHAPES[shape]
    rho_total = rho_obs * n / (n - 2)
    u = np.sqrt(n) * (np.sqrt(rho_total / (1 + rho_total)) - s["R"]) / np.sqrt(s["var_R"])
    return float(min(1.0, max(0.0, sup_tail_envelope(u, k, s["lam"]))))


if __name__ == "__main__":
    from scipy.stats import chi
    for k in (1, 2, 3, 5, 12):  # A-T (4.5.15): sum_j L_j(S^{k-1}) rho_j(u) = P(chi_k >= u)
        for u in (1.0, 3.0, 6.0):
            assert abs(float(sup_tail(u, k, 1.0, even=False)) - chi.sf(u, k)) < 1e-9 * max(1, chi.sf(u, k)) + 1e-12, (k, u)
    assert abs(sup_p_rho_scale(SHAPES["gaussian"]["b"] + 2.326 * sigma_rho("gaussian") / 10, 100, 1, "gaussian") - 0.01) < 1e-3
    r = SHAPES["gaussian"]["R"] + 2.326 * np.sqrt(SHAPES["gaussian"]["var_R"]) / 10
    assert abs(sup_p(r * r / (1 - r * r) * 98 / 100, 100, 1, "gaussian") - 0.01) < 1e-3
    for k in (6, 9, 12):  # the envelope never gives a smaller p at a larger rho, and p = 1 at the ceiling
        ps = [sup_p(r, 229, k) for r in np.linspace(3.0, 30.0, 60)]
        assert all(a >= b for a, b in zip(ps, ps[1:])) and ps[0] == 1.0, (k, ps[:5])
    assert abs(sigma_rho("gaussian") ** 2 - 6.5824) < 1e-3 and abs(sigma_rho("ceiling3") ** 2 - 19.2) < 1e-9
    print("self-check passed", round(sigma_rho("gaussian") ** 2, 4), round(sigma_rho("ceiling3") ** 2, 4),

# ===== check.py =====
"""protocol.md C1: null calibration of the sup-based p against the best flat cut of simulated single populations."""
import json
import sys
from multiprocessing import Pool
from pathlib import Path

import numpy as np

HERE = Path(__file__).resolve().parent
sys.path.insert(0, str(HERE))
sys.path.insert(0, str(HERE.parent / "gaussian_cut_rule_20261108"))
from count_rule import sup_p, sup_p_rho_scale  # noqa: E402
from cut_rule import cut_bound_p, gaussian_bound, unimodal_bound  # noqa: E402

REPS, NS = int(sys.argv[2]) if len(sys.argv) > 2 else 400, (100, 400, 1600)
SEED = int(sys.argv[3]) if len(sys.argv) > 3 else 0  # 0: first run; 1: fresh replication of the amended rule
CASES = [("gaussian", 1), ("gaussian", 2), ("gaussian", 3), ("ceiling3", 1), ("ceiling3", 3)]


def draw(shape, k, n, rng):
    if shape == "gaussian":
        return rng.standard_normal((n, k))
    if k == 1:
        return rng.uniform(-np.sqrt(3), np.sqrt(3), (n, 1))
    z = rng.standard_normal((n, 3))
    return np.sqrt(3) * z / np.linalg.norm(z, axis=1, keepdims=True)  # uniform on the sphere: uniform marginals


def directions(k, rng):
    if k == 1:
        return np.ones((1, 1))
    if k == 2:
        a = np.linspace(0, np.pi, 720, endpoint=False)
        return np.c_[np.cos(a), np.sin(a)]
    i = np.arange(2000) + 0.5  # Fibonacci points on the half sphere
    z = i / 2000
    phi = np.pi * (1 + 5 ** 0.5) * i
    r = np.sqrt(1 - z * z)
    return np.c_[r * np.cos(phi), r * np.sin(phi), z]


def best_cut(y, v):
    """(R^2, order, i) of the best threshold along v; y whitened and centred (total covariance, divisor n)."""
    n = len(y)
    order = np.argsort(y @ v)
    prefix = np.cumsum(y[order], axis=0)[:-1]
    sizes = np.arange(1, n)
    r2 = np.einsum("ij,ij->i", prefix, prefix) / (sizes * (n - sizes))
    i = int(np.argmax(r2))
    return r2[i], order, i + 1


def sup_rho(x, rng):
    n, k = x.shape
    y = x - x.mean(0)
    y = y @ np.linalg.inv(np.linalg.cholesky(y.T @ y / n)).T
    proj = y @ directions(k, rng).T
    order = np.argsort(proj, axis=0)
    best = (-1.0, None, None)
    for j in range(proj.shape[1]):
        prefix = np.cumsum(y[order[:, j]], axis=0)[:-1]
        sizes = np.arange(1, n)
        r2 = np.einsum("ij,ij->i", prefix, prefix) / (sizes * (n - sizes))
        i = int(np.argmax(r2))
        if r2[i] > best[0]:
            best = (r2[i], order[:, j], i + 1)
    for _ in range(5 if k > 1 else 0):  # Fisher re-sort: the best split's own direction, then its best threshold
        left = np.zeros(n, bool)
        left[best[1][:best[2]]] = True
        d = y[left].mean(0) - y[~left].mean(0)
        cand = best_cut(y, d / np.linalg.norm(d))
        if cand[0] <= best[0] + 1e-12:
            break
        best = cand
    r2 = best[0]
    left = np.zeros(n, bool)
    left[best[1][:best[2]]] = True
    return (n - 2) / n * r2 / (1 - r2), left


def task(job):
    shape, k, n = job
    rng = np.random.default_rng([k, n, 1 if shape == "gaussian" else 2] + ([SEED] if SEED else []))
    bound = gaussian_bound if shape == "gaussian" else unimodal_bound
    rows = []
    for _ in range(REPS):
        x = draw(shape, k, n, rng)
        rho, left = sup_rho(x, rng)
        old, _ = cut_bound_p(x, left, bound)
        rows.append({"shape": shape, "k": k, "n": n, "rho": rho, "p_new": sup_p(rho, n, k, shape), "p_rho_scale": sup_p_rho_scale(rho, n, k, shape), "p_old": old})
    return rows


if __name__ == "__main__":
    jobs = [(s, k, n) for s, k in CASES for n in NS]
    with Pool(int(sys.argv[1])) as pool, (HERE / ("check.jsonl" if not SEED else f"check_seed{SEED}.jsonl")).open("w") as f:
        for rows in pool.imap_unordered(task, jobs):
            f.write("".join(json.dumps(r) + "\n" for r in rows))
            r = rows[0]
            p = np.array([x["p_new"] for x in rows])
            po = np.array([x["p_old"] for x in rows])
            print(r["shape"], r["k"], r["n"], "new<=.01", round((p <= 0.01).mean(), 4), "new<=.05", round((p <= 0.05).mean(), 4),
                  "old<=.05", round((po <= 0.05).mean(), 4), "mean rho", round(np.mean([x["rho"] for x in rows]), 3), flush=True)

# ===== check_highk.py =====
"""Added after the endpoints (deviations.md): null check at k = 6 and 9, where the real clusters sit.

Gaussian null with the Gaussian rule; product of uniforms (best cuts at the axes, rho* = 3) with the ceiling-3 rule.
The sup is searched (300 random directions, the 10 best refined by Fisher re-sorts), so it is a lower bound.
"""
import json
import sys
from multiprocessing import Pool
from pathlib import Path

import numpy as np

HERE = Path(__file__).resolve().parent
sys.path.insert(0, str(HERE))
from check import best_cut  # noqa: E402
from count_rule import sup_p  # noqa: E402

REPS = 300


def sup_rho(x, rng):
    n, k = x.shape
    y = x - x.mean(0)
    y = y @ np.linalg.inv(np.linalg.cholesky(y.T @ y / n)).T
    v = rng.standard_normal((300, k))
    starts = sorted((best_cut(y, d / np.linalg.norm(d)) for d in v), key=lambda c: -c[0])[:10]
    best = 0.0
    for cand in starts:
        for _ in range(10):
            left = np.zeros(n, bool)
            left[cand[1][:cand[2]]] = True
            d = y[left].mean(0) - y[~left].mean(0)
            new = best_cut(y, d / np.linalg.norm(d))
            if new[0] <= cand[0] + 1e-12:
                break
            cand = new
        best = max(best, cand[0])
    return (n - 2) / n * best / (1 - best)


def task(job):
    shape, k, n = job
    rng = np.random.default_rng([k, n, 3 if shape == "gaussian" else 4])
    rows = []
    for _ in range(REPS):
        x = rng.standard_normal((n, k)) if shape == "gaussian" else rng.uniform(-1, 1, (n, k))
        rho = sup_rho(x, rng)
        rows.append({"shape": shape, "k": k, "n": n, "rho": rho, "p": sup_p(rho, n, k, shape)})
    return rows


if __name__ == "__main__":
    jobs = [(s, k, n) for s in ("gaussian", "ceiling3") for k in (6, 9) for n in (400, 1600)]
    with Pool(int(sys.argv[1])) as pool, (HERE / "check_highk.jsonl").open("w") as f:
        for rows in pool.imap_unordered(task, jobs):
            f.write("".join(json.dumps(r) + "\n" for r in rows))
            p = np.array([r["p"] for r in rows])
            print(rows[0]["shape"], rows[0]["k"], rows[0]["n"], "p<=.01", round((p <= 0.01).mean(), 4), "p<=.05",
                  round((p <= 0.05).mean(), 4), "mean rho", round(np.mean([r["rho"] for r in rows]), 3), flush=True)

HERE = Path(__file__).resolve().parent

from count_rule import sup_p  # noqa: E402

TRACE = HERE.parent / "ceiling_rule_real_20261109"
pd.set_option("display.width", 220)


def needed_rho(n, k):
    """Smallest rho with p <= 0.01 (p is non-increasing in rho); inf when even R -> 1 is not enough."""
    if sup_p(1e9, n, k) > 0.01:
        return np.inf
    lo, hi = 3.0, 1e9
    for _ in range(200):
        mid = np.sqrt(lo * hi)
        lo, hi = (lo, mid) if sup_p(mid, n, k) <= 0.01 else (mid, hi)
    return hi


t = pd.read_csv(TRACE / "trace_pairs.csv")
t["needed_sup"] = [needed_rho(r.cluster_node_cells, int(r.top_k)) if r.top_k == r.top_k else np.nan for r in t.itertuples()]
t["passes_sup"] = t.top_rho >= t.needed_sup
print(t[["pair", "cluster_node_cells", "top_k", "top_rho", "top_needed_rho", "needed_sup", "passes_sup", "clean_boundary"]]
      .round(2).to_string(index=False))
print("\n27 pairs: top split passes the sup rule:", int(t.passes_sup.sum()), "| clean boundary and passes:",

      round(t.top_needed_rho.max(), 2), "new", round(t.needed_sup.min(), 2), "-", round(t.needed_sup.max(), 2), "\n")

# ===== conservativeness.py =====
"""Added after the endpoints: null 99th percentile of the best cut vs the rho each rule needs (k = 6, 9)."""
import sys

import numpy as np
import pandas as pd
from scipy.stats import ncx2

sys.path.insert(0, "../gaussian_cut_rule_20261108")
from count_rule import SHAPES, sup_p  # noqa: E402
from cut_rule import log_flat_cuts  # noqa: E402


def needed(fn):
    lo, hi = 1.0, 1e9
    for _ in range(200):
        mid = np.sqrt(lo * hi)
        lo, hi = (lo, mid) if fn(mid) <= 0.01 else (mid, hi)
    return hi


d = pd.read_json("check_highk.jsonl", lines=True)
for (shape, k, n), g in d.groupby(["shape", "k", "n"]):
    b = SHAPES[shape]["b"]
    new = needed(lambda r: sup_p(r, n, k, shape))
    old = needed(lambda r: min(1.0, np.exp(log_flat_cuts(n, k) + ncx2.logsf(n * r, k, n * b))))
    print(f"{shape:8s} k={k} n={n}: null best-cut rho 99th pct {np.quantile(g.rho, .99):.2f} (max {g.rho.max():.2f}) "
          f"| needed: sup rule {new:.2f}, flat-cut count {old:.2f}")
